# Resume Fact Bank

The single source of truth for tailored resumes. Every claim in a tailored resume must trace to an entry here.
Rephrasing, reordering, and selecting are fine; adding skills, numbers, titles, or outcomes that aren't here is not.

Copy to `~/.jobsearch/resume/fact-bank.md`. Build it from your existing resumes, then keep adding confirmed facts (with dates) as tailoring surfaces them.
